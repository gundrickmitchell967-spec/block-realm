SETTINGS

Mouse: look · WASD: move · Space: jump · Shift: run
Left click: break a block · Right click: place the selected block
1-8 or mouse wheel: choose an inventory slot · E: block catalog (add or remove blocks)
Right click a door: open or close it · Right click a bed at night: go to sleep
M: sound effects on or off · Esc: pause and save
Double-tap Space: fly (Space up, Shift down) · T: chat with other players (multiplayer)

PRIVATE

Enter the code to unlock your private settings.

Wall hacks let you see other players through walls and blocks.

While you are invisible, other players cannot see you or your name, and you do not appear on their screens.

BLOCK CATALOG

Click a block below to add it to your inventory. Click a slot to pick it, then add a block to replace it. Click the red × to remove a block.
