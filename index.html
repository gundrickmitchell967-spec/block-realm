HOW TO PLAY

Mouse: look · WASD: move · Space: jump · Shift: run
Left click: break a block · Right click: place the selected block
1-8 or mouse wheel: choose an inventory slot · E: block catalog (add or remove blocks)
Right click a door: open or close it · H at a door: set or open its password (multiplayer)
N: map of the land near you · Right click a bed at night: go to sleep · Right click the shopkeeper: open the shop · Right click a slot machine in the casino: play
Cutting land: hold the Land Surveyor. Right-click one corner block inside your plot, then right-click the opposite corner.
A yellow outline shows the part. Press CUT, name the new plot, and it becomes its own plot. Your other land stays yours.
M: sound effects on or off · Esc: pause and save
Double-tap Space: fly (Space up, Shift down) · T: chat with other players (multiplayer)
V: talk to players close to you (voice chat, multiplayer)

Touch: drag the circle on the left to walk (double tap it while flying to go fast), drag anywhere else to look.
Tap anywhere to place the selected block there. Hold still on a block to break it. BREAK breaks the block in the middle of the screen. JUMP jumps. BAG opens the block catalog. DOOR (shown when you look at a door) sets or opens its password. MENU pauses.

CASINO

LUCKY 7
???

Three SEVENs pays 60 times your bet. Three of anything else pays 8 times. Two alike gets your bet back. Otherwise the bet is lost.

The wheel has 30 segments: 0x (the bet is lost) most of the time, 1x (the bet comes back), 2x, 3x, 5x and a rare 10x.

SHOP

Pick a job and start a shift. Each minute pays part of the job's cash: half for moving around, half for building or breaking blocks. Standing still earns nothing. A shift is followed by a 5 minute break.

Sign in once a day for a reward. Each day in a row pays more, up to day 7. Missing a day starts the count again.

Playtime: every 10 minutes you play (moving around, not standing idle) banks 20 cash. Up to 3 rewards can wait for you.

Buy an investment once. It earns cash while you are away from the game, up to 12 hours of income waiting to be collected.

A bounty is cash someone puts on a player. Smack that player (aim at them and press F) to take it: they stand still for 15 seconds. Then come back here to claim it.

HOW TO GET CASH

Find bugs in the game: the bigger the bug, the more cash you get.

Find glitches in the game: you are paid for them too.

Build houses: the better a house is built, the easier it is to reach, and the more important it is, the more you get paid.

The game creator pays out cash for bugs, glitches and houses.
